# Code of Conduct

Happy2Help exists so that every student is heard. We ask the same of our community.

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent; disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination or demeaning language of any kind.
- Protect children: never share real student data, and treat safeguarding topics with care and seriousness.
- Give credit, cite sources, and be honest about what works and what does not.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this code. Report concerns to the
maintainers privately (see SECURITY.md for contact).

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
